Design Specifications

  • Concept: Tree of Life with balanced roots and branches symbolizing ancestral wisdom and growth
  • Symmetry: Perfectly balanced design representing stability and wholeness
  • Root System: As prominent as branches, honoring foundation and ancestry
  • Circular Frame: Subtle circular element suggesting unity and completeness
  • Scalability: Clean linework works from 24px (favicon) to any large size
  • Versions: Full color for web/print, monochrome for versatility

Symbolism

  • Canopy (Green): Growth, wisdom shared, reaching outward to community
  • Trunk (Terra Cotta): Strength, stability, connection between generations
  • Roots (Ochre): Ancestry, foundation, deep cultural knowledge
  • Circular Form: Wholeness, cycles of life, unity
  • Balance: Equal emphasis on past (roots) and future (branches)
